# Concept Brief

## 1. Working name
Task Board

## 2. The pitch, in one sentence
For PSA e-board members, Task Board centralizes task deadlines and assignments in one place, so we stop losing action items in the group chat.

## 3. Who it's for — specifically
E-board members (around 5 people, growing) who need to know what their upcoming tasks are, what the deadline is, and who else is handling what—without scrolling through a group chat where messages get buried.

## 4. The job it does
When we're planning an event or coordinating something for PSA, I want to see all tasks and their deadlines in one place, so I can actually follow up and know what's done.

## 5. What people do instead today
We discuss tasks and deadlines in a group chat, but messages get lost and forgotten. We end up asking "wait, who was handling this?" and can't track what's actually complete.

## 6. Your biggest unknown
Whether the e-board will actually use it consistently instead of defaulting back to the group chat when it gets busy.
